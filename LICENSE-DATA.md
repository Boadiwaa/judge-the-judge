## What this covers

- `data/cases/`: the 24 synthetic consultations, planted errors and answer key (severity, likelihood,
  rationale, subtle/obvious tags)
- `data/notes/`: the clean and seeded notes built from them
- `data/results/`: the computed metrics, matches and flags
- The study protocol text (the Protocol page and its Markdown download)

## What it does not cover

- **Code** (`src/`, `scripts/`, `tests/`, `Dockerfile`): MIT, see `LICENSE`.
- **Raw judge outputs** (`data/judge_outputs/`) are text generated by the models, shared for
  reproducibility. Gemma 4 outputs come from a model released under Apache 2.0. The single pilot
  output in `J1_medgemma-1.5-4b-it-Q3_K_M/` comes from MedGemma, which is under Google's Health AI
  Developer Foundations terms; reuse it under those terms.
- **Fonts** in `src/jtj/app/assets/fonts/`: SIL Open Font License 1.1, see the `OFL-*.txt` files there.
